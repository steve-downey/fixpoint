#+begin_abstract
Multiply a list of integers. The instant you see a zero, the answer is
zero --- so why examine the rest? A hylomorphism has no way to say "stop
generating"; an Elgot algebra gives the coalgebra exactly that veto, and
its dual gives the algebra the seed that produced each layer.
#+end_abstract

* elgot: The Coalgebra's Veto

~refold~'s coalgebra must always answer with a layer; the recursion stops
only when a layer has no children.
An Elgot coalgebra [cite:@elgot1975monadic] [cite:@adamek2006elgot]
answers with an ~either~: *Left, a finished result --- stop here, fold
nothing below*; *Right, one more layer --- continue*.
Part 4's convention, doing hylomorphism duty.
From [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/elgot.hpp][=src/smd/fixpoint/elgot.hpp=]]:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/elgot.hpp::e780b556-5251-4f23-8b5a-5634e36439a9]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "e780b556-5251-4f23-8b5a-5634e36439a9 end"

~refold~ with a ~match~ wrapped around the step: Left returns the answer
directly --- note that the algebra is never called, not even once, if the
/first/ seed short-circuits; Right proceeds exactly as ~refold~ would.

The example is the zero-in-a-product bailout, from
[[file:../../src/examples/elgot_shortcircuit.cpp][=src/examples/elgot_shortcircuit.cpp=]]:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/examples/elgot_shortcircuit.cpp::5e2768c7-a257-4616-aef1-6d7634d818a2]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "5e2768c7-a257-4616-aef1-6d7634d818a2 end"

The output is the interesting part:

#+begin_example
values: 6 elements
product (bails out at the first 0): 0
elements examined: 3 / 6
#+end_example

The examined-count is not decoration --- it is the /only/ observable
evidence of the short-circuit.
The product alone proves nothing: multiplying by zero swallows whether
the tail was visited.
The header makes the same point about testing, and it generalizes: a
plausible off-by-one --- expand one extra layer, then discard it ---
still gets every numeric answer right, so the test suite counts coalgebra
invocations instead of trusting outputs.
When a scheme's contract is about /work avoided/, test the work, not the
answer.

* coelgot: The Algebra Gets the Seed

Dualize once more.
Where ~elgot~ strengthens the coalgebra, ~coelgot~ strengthens the
algebra: alongside the already-folded children, it receives /the seed
that produced the current layer/:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/elgot.hpp::47505502-2ee0-4088-8753-3bf71df74360]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "47505502-2ee0-4088-8753-3bf71df74360 end"

No short-circuit here --- the extra power is context, not control.
It is ~para~'s move transplanted to hylomorphisms: ~para~'s algebra saw
the original subtree next to each result; ~coelgot~'s sees the original
/seed/ next to the folded layer, in a refold where no subtree ever
exists to show.

One implementation note earns its comment: ~coalgebra(seed)~ is bound to
a local before the ~fmap~.
A careless transcription that calls ~psi~ twice --- once to build the
layer, once inside the fold --- would be invisible to every test with a
pure coalgebra and would silently double the cost, and the side effects,
of an impure one.
The elgot/coelgot pair is small; the discipline in the comments is most
of its value.

* The Pattern Completing

Count the extra-power moves the catalog has made:

- give the *algebra* more input: para (subtree), zygo (helper), histo
  (history), coelgot (seed);
- give the *coalgebra* more output: apo (graft-or-seed), futu (chunk),
  elgot (answer-or-layer).

Every one is "same three-line worker, richer type at one position", and
each side's riches line up with the other's --- products and annotations
feeding algebras, sums and chunks flowing from coalgebras.
The catalog is complete except for the reveal: Part 11 shows the one
construction all the algebra-side schemes are instances of, the one all
the coalgebra-side schemes are instances of, and the single moving part
--- a distributive law --- that selects among them.
