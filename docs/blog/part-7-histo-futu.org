#+begin_abstract
Coin-change dynamic programming needs ~minCoins(n-1)~, ~minCoins(n-4)~, and
~minCoins(n-5)~ at once; a plain fold sees only its immediate child.
Run-length decoding wants to emit a whole run of list cells in one step; a
plain unfold emits exactly one layer per call. Course-of-values recursion
fixes both, and the carriers that do it --- Cofree and Free --- are the
most interesting data structures in the library.
#+end_abstract

* Cofree: A Tree Annotated With Its Own Past

The pair carrier of Part 5 gave a fold one extra value per child.
Course-of-values recursion [cite:@uustalu1999primitive] needs /every/
value ever computed below a child.
The carrier for that is the Cofree comonad: the same shape as the tree
being folded, but every node also holds an annotation.
From [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/cofree.hpp][=src/smd/fixpoint/cofree.hpp=]]:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/cofree.hpp::bfa61c28-7fe2-46f9-8ca6-1006b3348836]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "bfa61c28-7fe2-46f9-8ca6-1006b3348836 end"

Notice that this compiles for the same reason ~Fix~ did.
~F~'s recursive positions are boxed, so ~F<Cofree<F, A>>~ is a complete
type while ~Cofree<F, A>~ is still being defined --- the Part 1 trick again.
(And the hand-written ~operator==~ is the Part 1 problem again, at the
type where Clang's eager completeness check actually fired.)

* histo: Fold With Total Recall

From [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/histo.hpp][=src/smd/fixpoint/histo.hpp=]]:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/histo.hpp::5ca376cb-3a39-4e25-bbd9-999bb5096014]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "5ca376cb-3a39-4e25-bbd9-999bb5096014 end"

Four lines, and the same construction as zygo: a ~fold_fix~ at a richer
carrier.
The combined algebra runs the user's algebra, then stores the layer
/itself/ as the new node's tail --- so each child position accumulates its
entire annotated history as the fold climbs.
The user's algebra receives ~F<Cofree<F, Result>>~: at every child, the
head is that child's result, and the tail goes all the way down.
~extract~ at the root throws the scaffolding away.

The classic use is dynamic programming where the recurrence skips levels.
From [[file:../../src/examples/histo_coin_change.cpp][=src/examples/histo_coin_change.cpp=]]:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/examples/histo_coin_change.cpp::7a78616c-c66c-4ffd-ab18-e192aac2d913]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "7a78616c-c66c-4ffd-ab18-e192aac2d913 end"

~minCoins(n)~ needs ~minCoins(n-1)~, ~minCoins(n-4)~, ~minCoins(n-5)~.
The predecessor's head is the first; ~look_back~ walks the history 3 and 4
more steps for the others.
Nothing is ever recomputed --- the history /is/ the memo table, built by
the fold itself, no map, no mutable array.
The program prints ~minCoins(8) = 2~ (4+4) and ~minCoins(12) = 3~ (4+4+4),
the cases where a greedy or naive-recursive formulation goes wrong or goes
exponential.

* Free: Computation Chunks With Seeds at the Leaves

Dualize the annotation idea.
Cofree is /always/ a node, /plus/ an annotation at every level.
Its mirror is /either/ a value /or/ a layer of more structure --- the Free
monad.
From [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/free.hpp][=src/smd/fixpoint/free.hpp=]]:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/free.hpp::11de0b77-f3c3-413b-9949-5bc28a2430c2]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "11de0b77-f3c3-413b-9949-5bc28a2430c2 end"

Read ~Free<F, Seed>~ as "a chunk of tree, with fresh seeds where I stopped
building."
~pure_free~ is a seed; ~roll_free~ is one more layer of chunk.
Cofree is the cofree /comonad/ over F and Free the free /monad/ --- the
library registers those instances, and Part 11 spends them.

* futu: Unfold Several Layers per Step

An ~unfold_fix~ coalgebra returns ~F<Seed>~: one layer, seeds under it.
A ~futu~ coalgebra returns ~F<Free<F, Seed>>~: one layer whose children
are whole /chunks/ [cite:@uustalu1999primitive].
From [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/futu.hpp][=src/smd/fixpoint/futu.hpp=]]:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/smd/fixpoint/futu.hpp::e0f553ab-ab72-44c9-b21c-ab1aff17751a]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "e0f553ab-ab72-44c9-b21c-ab1aff17751a end"

The worker walks a chunk: a ~Pure~ leaf resumes the coalgebra at its seed;
a ~Roll~ layer becomes real tree, one ~wrap_fix~ per layer, recursing into
child chunks.
~futu~ itself is ~unfold_fix~ with the worker spliced in where the plain
recursive call was.

Run-length decoding is the shape made obvious, from
[[file:../../src/examples/futu_rle_decode.cpp][=src/examples/futu_rle_decode.cpp=]]:

#+transclude: [[file:../../src/examples/futu_rle_decode.cpp::5108825f-15af-4371-a949-3ecb9e370b8d]] :lines 2- :src cpp :end "5108825f-15af-4371-a949-3ecb9e370b8d end"

One coalgebra call sees ~(count, value)~ and emits ~count~ ~Cons~ layers
as a single nested-~roll_free~ chunk, with the ~Pure~ leaf carrying the
index of the next pair.
~[(2,7), (3,1)]~ decodes to ~[7, 7, 1, 1, 1]~ with three coalgebra calls,
not five.
A plain unfold would need seed bookkeeping --- "which pair, how many
emitted so far" --- to fake what the chunk states directly.

* The Symmetry Table

The catalog is now symmetric three levels deep; here is the table:

| fold side                          | unfold side                     |
|------------------------------------+---------------------------------|
| ~fold_fix~ --- child's result      | ~unfold_fix~ --- one seed       |
| ~para~ --- result + original (pair) | ~apo~ --- seed or subtree (either) |
| ~histo~ --- full history (Cofree)  | ~futu~ --- full chunk (Free)    |

Products and annotations on the left; sums and chunks on the right.
Pair generalizes to Cofree, either generalizes to Free --- and each right
column entry is the categorical dual of its left neighbor.
This table is not a mnemonic; it is a theorem, and Part 11 proves it in
code: every left entry is ~gcata~ at a comonad, every right entry ~gana~
at a monad.
But first, the fused forms --- what happens when a histo runs directly on
a seed --- and two schemes that step outside the functor framework
entirely.
