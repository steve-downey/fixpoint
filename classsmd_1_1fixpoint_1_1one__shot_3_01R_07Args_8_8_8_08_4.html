◆ operator()()

template<class R , class... Args>
auto smd::fixpoint::one_shot< R(Args...)>::operator() ( Args...  args) && -> R
inline

Invoke the stored target and consume it – the target is destroyed as part of this call whether or not it throws. Precondition: this one_shot currently holds a target (a default-constructed, moved-from, or already-called one_shot does not) – asserted, so a violation is a hard, diagnosable error rather than a UB-adjacent null/moved-from call.
